# My Favorite Movie 

## Sense and Sensibility

**Sense and Sensibility** is my favorite movie. It is a romantic drama based on the novel by Jane Austen.

- **Genre:** Romance and Drama
- **Author:** Jane Austen
- **Favorite part:** The love story and the beautiful costumes.

*I love this movie because it teaches us about love, family, patience, and understanding.*

> "Love and feelings are important, but so are sense and sensibility."
